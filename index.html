the lore, abridged

i am not a startup.
i’m a guy with wifi.

it starts at 2am with a normal idea. then i describe it badly to Freebuff, it says “sure, why not”, and eight hours later something installs on your phone.

no framework, no meetings, no roadmap, no investors. just vanilla JavaScript, a healthy relationship with the browser console, and the belief that free software should feel nice to use.

every app here is free, has no ads, no accounts and no analytics. i can’t even see you reading this page. that’s the whole business model.

the process

how i vibe (patent pending)

people ask how the apps get made. they assume a team. it’s one person, one AI, and a suspicious amount of orange juice.

  1. 01

    have a normal idea at 2am

    “what if studying japanese felt like feeding a pet?” sounds normal at 2am. it is not.

  2. 02

    explain it badly to freebuff

    half sentence, three typos, one reference to a meme. somehow it understands.

  3. 03

    fix 47 errors, call them features

    the bug becomes a “design decision”. the crash becomes “aggressive battery saving”.

  4. 04

    ship it free, go to sleep

    github pages, direct APK, no store review queue. repeat until the folder is full of apps.
